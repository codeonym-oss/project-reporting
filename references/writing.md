# Writing a report

The template says what a report holds. These rules hold for every format.

1. **From `.work/` only.** A statement that no item supports is not written. Nothing under Gaps is ever written.
2. **For the reader named in the template,** in words they use. Say what a person can do or see, not how it is built:
   "a blocked conversation frees itself", never the mechanism.
3. **Before, Now, Gain** are what was true, what is true, and what that brings. One sentence each. A gain is
   something the reader would notice; "cleaner code" is not one.
4. **A parent stands for its children** that landed in the period. Say it is complete once the last one landed.
5. **No filler:** no introduction, no summary of the summary, no praise, no future promises.
6. **Keep the template's limits.** When there is too much, propose what to merge or to make one line, and let the
   person choose.
7. **A longer period is not a longer report:** main results by area, one line for the rest.

## Demos

The best available, per item:

| kind | when | in the report |
|---|---|---|
| video | a video matches the item or its parent | `▶ [Video](path)` and its length |
| screenshots | visible, not worth a video | one or two images from the period's `assets/` |
| explained | it can't be shown on a screen | one concrete scenario: what you do, what happened before, what happens now |
| none | one-line items | nothing |

- Videos are linked, never copied into `.tracking/`. Screenshots go in the period's `assets/`.
- A click path that no video or screenshot supports is written as explained, never as an instruction to follow.
- Put links, keys and the production status on one line starting with `▶`; `check` doesn't count its words.

## Languages

Write the lead language first and have it approved. Write the others from the same items and Context, in natural
language, not sentence by sentence from the lead. Carry every correction made to the lead over to the others.
