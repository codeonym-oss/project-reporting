# 011: Meeting notes are a source, with no recap

**Date:** 2026-11-23 (grilling, Q11; agreed: "lets go with c")

**Question:** what happens after the meeting?

**Decision:** **an optional `notes.md` in a period folder is read by the next `collect`.** A request or promise becomes a dated finding on its item, or a line in the period's `items.md` when it concerns none. No recap is generated.

- **Why:** a client's own request is the strongest "why" a later report can give.
- **Not chosen:** a generated recap (a format can add one); ignoring notes.
