# report

## Problem

Collected work has to become a short report a client can follow.

## Change

- `report` with periods, formats and languages (decisions 006, 008, 010).
- Demo proposals and the hand-over to flow-reel (decision 007).
- Exports on request and publishing after a yes (decision 008).

## Out of scope

Scheduled runs, dashboards, hours or cost reports.

## Done when

- The person would show the trial week's report to their client as it is.
- `check` passes on it.
- A second format is created by asking, and a month is written from the same `.work/`.
- The other languages follow a correction made to the lead report.
